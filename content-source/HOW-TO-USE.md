# Portfolio content intake

Drop anything from the current UXfolio site in here. Nothing in this folder gets published —
it's raw source material that I read and translate into the case studies in
`~/Documents/portfolio/src/content/work/`.

## What to drop

**Screenshots of the UXfolio pages** — into this folder or `images/`.
Name them so the project is obvious: `campaign-creation-01.png`, `lexio-hero.png`, etc.
Full-page captures beat viewport crops; I need the whole scroll, not just the hero.

**Product screens / designs** — into `images/`.
These become the actual figures inside the case studies. Before/after pairs, flow diagrams
and annotated screens are the most useful. Same naming convention.

**Body copy as text** — one `.md` or `.txt` per project, e.g. `campaign-creation.txt`.

## One important caveat on screenshots

Screenshots are ideal for layout, visual style, project lists and short copy.

For **long paragraphs of your writing**, please paste the text rather than only screenshotting
it. Transcribing prose out of an image is error-prone, and if I get a word wrong I'd be putting
words in your mouth in a document that represents you. Text files guarantee your wording
survives verbatim. Screenshots for the visuals, text for the prose.

## Faster alternative worth trying first

Your custom domain `markpachucki.info` fails its TLS handshake, but UXfolio also serves sites on
its own subdomain (something like `yourname.uxfol.io`). If that URL loads in your browser, send
it to me — I can pull every case study directly and skip this folder entirely.

## What happens next

Once there's material here I'll produce a content inventory: project by project, what copy
exists, which metrics are present, and which staff-level gaps remain (missing impact numbers,
missing "what I owned", missing the problem reframe). Then I migrate it into the MDX template
and resolve the 61 `[NEEDS INPUT]` markers.
