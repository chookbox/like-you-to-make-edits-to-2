## A bit about myself

Growing up in rural Virginia, I didn't have much stimulus so I had to make my own fun. From an early age, I was fascinated with computers, electronic gadgets, and the possibilities they unlocked. You could often find me out in the woods, shooting videos on my 2 megapixel digital camera, then editing the footage on our Windows 98 computer while learning the ins & outs of various creative software.

That upbringing really jumpstarted my curiosity with the outside world and the potential of using computers to bring it to life & share with others in a variety of ways. Through a serendipitous path of elective courses in college, I discovered the field of Design & knew that it was my calling. Since then, I've pursued Design in all it's different forms without looking back.

## A few other facts about me:

- I received a scholarship to Virginia Tech in Photography and was the first student to study both Business & Design.
- I love to travel. So much so that I left my job in 2017 to backpack around the world for a year. Some highlights include: volunteering at an elephant sanctuary in Thailand, hiking to Mt. Everest Basecamp, and doing a Vipassanā course in Nepal where I meditated in silence for 10 days.
- What I love most about Design is the power it has to help people by saving them time & effort (the most valuable resources in the world).
- Outside of work, I enjoy exploring Berlin, playing soccer, going to concerts, photography, snowboarding, & learning new things.
