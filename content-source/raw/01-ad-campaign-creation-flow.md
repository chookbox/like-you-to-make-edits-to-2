## Some background on Delivery Hero and the role of AdTech.

Delivery Hero is a global food delivery platform that owns 17+ different food delivery brands across 70+ countries. At this scale, they process over 11 million orders per day (127 orders per second 🤯) making it the top food delivery company in the world.

However, profit margins are pretty slim in the food delivery business and they must rely on other revenue sources, such as advertising, to boost profitability.

AdTech is the main driver behind the company's profitability thanks to an average monthly revenue of €700 million. Within AdTech, I am the lead designer of the most valuable product, Sponsored Listings, which restaurants can use to promote their business in the apps.

## What was the problem with the ad creation page?

When I joined in 2023, we had a basic page in place for restaurant owners to book their own ads and elevate their listing higher on our apps.

Restaurant owners found the ad campaign creation process cumbersome, leading to low adoption rates and missed revenue opportunities.

## What did we learn about our users' needs & pain points through research?

As the lead designer for this product, I decided to first gather all of the insights we had from user interviews. This also included our internal objectives because after all, we need this advertising engine to power our own business too.

The key user questions I identified were:

- What's the value of this for me?
- What are the best settings to use?
- What do these complex terms mean?
- Which specific areas should we focus on improving?

Based on those findings above, we decided to focus on a few key areas that we believed would address those needs above and have the biggest impact improving our users' confidence leading to better conversion:

- Add basic onboarding content to the page
- Provide smart recommendations for each setting
- Make the "bid" setting less intimidating
- Show performance estimates of what the user can expect from their ad
- Lift the primary CTA above the fold so it's always visible

## Workshop time

The lead PM, myself, another designer, and my design manager met for a half-day workshop and we aligned on a general direction for the new design by doing the following:

- Analyzing competitor designs
- Sketching our own ideas independently (divergent activity)
- Sharing our designs with each other
- Discussing which aspects of our designs we think would work best
- Combining those features into a single vision (convergent activity)

## Iteration & increasing fidelity

With the vision in place, it was my turn to go into "focus mode" and explore certain details such as:

- Arranging into a 2-column layout
- Include the most important information a user needs to know as onboarding content
- Simplifying & de-emphasizing the bid price setting
- Displaying performance metrics

## Arriving at the final design

- 3 onboarding cards across the top to help establish a stronger mental model for users
- Smart default (recommended) selections
- Placing the bid price option within an "Advanced Settings" section to prevent novices from being intimidated but still allowing experts to customize
- Renaming "bid" to a more friendly and practical term, "price per menu view"
- Using the term "Automatic" for the default bid price to reassure users and make their ads competitive ("set it and forget it")
- Estimated performance shown on the right to give users more confidence
- Elevating the "Start Campaign" button above the fold for better visibility
