**Subtitle:** Here are a few smaller projects that I've led from a design perspective during my career.

## Delivery Hero Chart Guidelines

I led the reporting workstream within the design team where we crafted how reporting pages and dashbaords at Delivery Hero should be designed. One key deliverable from our workstream was Chart Guidelines that provide a framework for data visualization.

**My role:** As the leader of this workstream of 5 designers, I created an outline and structure for these guidelines for the other members to help me fill out. I began by collecting all the current charts across our restaurant portal and created basic guidelines for when to use each type and the specs of how they should look. After we published our guidelines, I worked alongside engineers to apply these guidelines to our global chart library.

**Timeline:** 3 months

**Result:** An interactive Figma prototype that anyone at the company can easily access and learn about which chart is best for their use case, how it should look, and its interactions.

## Star Rating Component for Delivery Hero's Design System

In 2024, a design team at Delivery Hero set out to create a new design system to unite all of its products around the world and raise the bar on their design language. As part of this effort, they recruited designers from around the company to help build this system.

**My role:** I was a contributor to the new design system and it was my responsibility to create and launch a Star Rating component for users on Delivery Hero's apps so they can rate restaurants and their other experiences on the app. I researched benchmarks, collected uses cases, and created detailed specs for this component including animations.

**Timeline:** 2 months

**Result:** A dev-ready Star Rating component for any team at the company to implement quickly and easily. I also wrote detailed documentation so designers know when to use which variants of the component for their use cases.

## McMaster-Carr Design Handbook

Despite having an excellent, design-centric culture, McMaster had never formally published design standards until recently. In order to design more effectively, it's essential to have a shared understanding of your users and their needs as well as the objectives of the company.

**My role:** Worked alongside the design leadership team to outline and create the structure of the handbook. I personally created the design process and principles pages which outline the design process at McMaster and describe the design principles of the organization along with examples that embody those principles.

**Timeline:** 3 months

**Result:** A handbook that establishes process, brand, and design principles that compel designers to create solutions that delight, while providing the guardrails to ensure that designs work together to create a unified and recognizable experience.

## Customer Journey Map of Lexio

Bits & pieces of Lexio's customer journey only existed in people's heads and there was no single source of truth for us to collaborate on & improve. In early 2020, I set out to fix that.

**My role:** Organized various meetings across departments (Sales, Marketing, Operations, Client Service) to gather input on the Lexio Journey as it existed currently and then worked with the design team to organize the content of the map in a logical way. I then built out this digital map in Sketch, presented it to the various teams for review & feedback, and finalized the map with an accurate representation of what customers are thinking & feeling along the journey. I shared this map with the engineering teams to get everyone aligned on the current process and gathered feedback.

**Timeline:** 2 months

**Result:** A holistic view of the entire Lexio user journey that enabled all of us to zoom out and see which areas needed the most improvement. This allowed us to focus on the most impactful aspects and we set out to improve this journey in a variety of ways, from product messaging to data setup.

## Quill Tableau Extension

Quill is one of Narrative Science's older products. It's an extension that integrates into many of the most popular BI tools and brings data storytelling directly to existing dashboards. The Tableau version needed a redesign to comply with their new standards.

**My role:** Performed a heuristic evaluation of the current tool and identified key areas of improvement. I then redesigned each area of the extension in accordance with Tableau's latest extension standards while also improving the overall experience of the product. Lastly, I gathered stakeholder feedback on the new experience, made some final tweaks, and worked with engineers to build out the update.

**Timeline:** 1 month

**Result:** A dramatically improved experience of Narrative Science's most popular product which boosted sales and increased user engagement.

## Nielsen Flash

This system facilitates the on-boarding process (scheduling meetings, building orders, signing contracts etc.) for new clients.

**My role:** Designed a system that replaces the manual sales workflow and increases new client sales & leads.

**Timeline:** 6 months

**Result:** 80% improvement in lead retention, increased revenue, and an improved workflow process for the sales team.

## Nielsen Music Connect

This product displays a wide variety of music data (sales, streaming, social shares, radio plays, etc.) in an intuitive way for users to gain key insights from the music industry.

**My role:** Quickly built a prototype to be presented to Nielsen leadership in order to convince them to build Music Connect in-house as opposed to going with a 3rd party vendor.

**Timeline:** 2 weeks

**Result:** The Music Connect prototype was demoed to senior leadership with great success. Nielsen decided to build the product in-house and Music Connect is now a highly successful product.

## Nielsen Insight Studio Landing Page

Insight Studio is one of the most popular apps on Nielsen's data platform. The landing page needed a redesign based on user feedback and engagement metrics.

**My role:** Redesigned the landing page from the ground up taking inspiration from other popular business tools.

**Timeline:** 2 months

**Result:** A captivating experience that provides a great first impression along with easy ways for users to access their favorite reports & dashboards or create new ones. This led to better user engagement and higher feedback scores.

## Nielsen Platform Usage Tracking

With Nielsen's latest platform going live, their leadership team needed a way to keep tabs on its overall usage and measure how successful the launch was.

**My role:** Designed a simple, yet powerful dashboard that gives an overview of key usage data for the platform.

**Timeline:** 1 month

**Result:** An executive summary for Nielsen's leadership team to check frequently and stay up to date on the latest metrics which allowed them to take action where needed.

## Nielsen API Guide

This application guides a user through the process of building an API that provides Nielsen data in real-time.

**Role:** Designed a guided experience that makes it easy for clients to build a Nielsen API to get the data they need.

**Timeline:** 6 months

**Result:** APIs that tap directly into Nielsen data are naturally complex. This streamlined experience empowers clients to create their own APIs that deliver data directly to their own BI tools.
