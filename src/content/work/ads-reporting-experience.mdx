## Context

<DeliveryHeroContext />

When I joined in 2023 we had a basic page for restaurant owners to view the performance of
their ad — metrics like the number of orders they received through it and how much it
generated in sales.

I identified a number of visual design issues to improve, and through research uncovered
several unmet needs:

- Restaurant owners with limited ad experience found the page **intimidating**
- Experienced advertisers wanted to see **aggregated performance** across multiple campaigns
  and restaurants
- Experienced advertisers were **missing key metrics**, breakdowns and visualizations

## Reframe

A common challenge for the design and product teams at Delivery Hero is creating a product
that's intuitive for small restaurant owners with limited technical knowledge but also
advanced enough for massive restaurant chains with dedicated marketers.

One page could not do both jobs. So rather than trying to balance the two audiences inside a
single report, I proposed we **change the information architecture**: add a new page giving a
high-level overview of ad performance, and link from it into more detailed reports for
advanced users.

That split is what made the rest of the work possible — the overview could get simpler and
more prescriptive precisely because the detail had somewhere else to live.

## Process

### The reporting landing page

I separated the three different ad types using a toggle at the top of the page. I initially
explored combining them into a single page, but each ad type serves a different purpose, and
since each is created separately it made sense to maintain that mental model in reporting
too.

The first section is a **recommendation system** giving users ways to improve their ads.
These "smart actions" make it easy to keep ads running, and they're also a key way Delivery
Hero increases ad revenue.

Finally I created a summary that uses **natural language** to tell restaurant owners how much
money their ads earn them for every euro spent — especially helpful for less experienced
owners judging whether their ads are worth what they pay.

### How the recommendation system works

Giving users recommendations on improving ad performance had been discussed for a while;
this was the opportunity to make it real.

I worked with my PM to identify a set of five recommendations we could provide. We then
worked with engineering to create a recommendation algorithm that surfaces only the most
applicable ones for a given ad's performance, and prioritizes the most impactful first.

The "add more budget" recommendation has been particularly beneficial for both restaurants
and Delivery Hero's AdTech revenue. **Over €1.1 million was added by restaurants around the
world through that card in Q4 2024.**

### The detailed report for experts

This page came with its own hurdles. We had a list of desired metrics and analysis from our
target users, but simply adding a dozen new metrics and four new features to the existing
page wasn't going to work.

I needed to rethink the layout of the entire page and how information should be grouped, to
keep the design clear while telling a complete story of advertising performance.

## Craft

Instead of a flat list, I categorized every metric into sections that build a narrative:

- **Campaign details** — moved to the top to make clear which campaign is being viewed, and
  to elevate the most common action, Edit
- **Sales** — how did my ad affect my business?
- **Visibility** — how many people saw my ad, and how many clicked it?
- **Customer insights** — what kind of customers did my ad bring me?
- **Conversion** — how effective was my ad in giving me more orders?
- **Performance by area** — how did my ad perform across the neighborhoods I serve?

Framing each group as the question it answers is what turned a metric dump into a report.

After validating those groups with users and internal stakeholders, I added new features:

- Custom time range
- Downloading the report
- Comparing performance to a previous period

- **Over €1.1 million** added by restaurants worldwide through the "add more budget"

- A five-recommendation smart actions system, ranked by an algorithm built with engineering
- A reporting architecture that serves first-time advertisers and dedicated marketers from
  the same product
