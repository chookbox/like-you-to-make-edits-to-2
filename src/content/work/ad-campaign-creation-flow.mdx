## Context

<DeliveryHeroContext />

When I joined in 2023, we had a basic page for restaurant owners to book their own ads and
elevate their listing higher in our apps. Restaurant owners found the campaign creation
process cumbersome, which led to low adoption rates and missed revenue opportunities.

## Reframe

**[DRAFTED — confirm or rewrite.]** The obvious reading was that the page was too complex
and needed simplifying. But the users weren't one audience: a restaurant owner booking their
first ad needs defaults and reassurance, while a chain with in-house marketers needs control.
Stripping the page would have served the first group by failing the second.

So the problem wasn't complexity, it was that the page exposed expert machinery to novices
by default. The bet: **keep every expert control, but demote it — and make the confident
default the visible path.** That reframing is what produced the "Advanced Settings"
treatment, the "Automatic" bid, and the renaming, rather than a simple cleanup.

## Process

As the lead designer I first gathered all the insights we already had from user interviews.
That also included our internal objectives — this advertising engine has to power our own
business too.

The key user questions I identified were:

- What's the value of this for me?
- What are the best settings to use?
- What do these complex terms mean?
- Which specific areas should we focus on improving?

Based on those findings we decided to focus on a few key areas we believed would address
those needs and have the biggest impact on our users' confidence, leading to better
conversion:

- Add basic onboarding content to the page
- Provide smart recommendations for each setting
- Make the "bid" setting less intimidating
- Show performance estimates of what the user can expect from their ad
- Lift the primary CTA above the fold so it's always visible

### Workshop

The lead PM, myself, another designer and my design manager met for a half-day workshop and
aligned on a general direction by:

- Analyzing competitor designs
- Sketching our own ideas independently — a divergent activity
- Sharing our designs with each other
- Discussing which aspects we thought would work best
- Combining those features into a single vision — a convergent activity

### Iteration and increasing fidelity

With the vision in place it was my turn to go into focus mode and explore the details:
arranging into a two-column layout, deciding what a user most needs to know as onboarding
content, simplifying and de-emphasizing the bid price setting, and displaying performance
metrics.

## Craft

The final design:

- **Three onboarding cards across the top** to establish a stronger mental model for users
- **Smart default (recommended) selections**
- **Bid price moved into "Advanced Settings"** — preventing novices from being intimidated
  while still allowing experts to customize
- **Renamed "bid"** to a friendlier, more practical term: *price per menu view*
- **"Automatic" as the default bid price**, reassuring users and keeping their ads
  competitive — set it and forget it
- **Estimated performance shown on the right** to give users more confidence
- **"Launch my campaign" elevated above the fold** for better visibility

The before and after are three months apart: April 2023 to June 2023.
